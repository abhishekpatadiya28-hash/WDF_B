📖 About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple and user-friendly student portal developed using HTML and CSS. It helps students organize their academic activities in one place. Students can check attendance, manage study notes, view assignments, monitor academic progress and maintain their profile through a clean and attractive interface.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make student life easier by providing a single platform where all important academic information is available in an organized and accessible way.

✨ Why StudentHub?

  • Easy access to Study Notes
  • Track Attendance
  • Manage Assignments
  • Student Profile
  • Daily Dashboard
  • Simple and Attractive Interface
